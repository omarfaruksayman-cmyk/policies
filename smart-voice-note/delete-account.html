Smart Voice Note: Delete Your Account

Last updated: 25 November 2026

1. Delete your account in the App

You can delete your account yourself, at any time, inside the App:

  • Open Smart Voice Note and go to Profile.
  • Tap Delete Account and confirm.
  • You are signed out, and your deletion request starts a 7-day waiting period.

You only need an account if you chose to sign in. If you never signed in, there is no account to delete.

2. What happens next

  • During the 7 days, if you sign in again, the deletion is cancelled automatically.
  • After 7 days, your account is permanently deleted, including your email address, plan data, password (if you set one), and feedback linked to your account.

3. What is not deleted

  • A one-way trial device marker (a hash of your device ID and the date a free trial was used). It is not linked to your account or email. It is kept only to prevent repeated free trials.
  • Data stored only on your phone (notes, history, settings, your API key). We never receive it. Clear the App's data in your phone settings or uninstall the App to remove it.
  • Your Google account and anything you shared with Google's Gemini service using your own API key. These are handled by Google under its own policies.

4. Cannot use the App? Request deletion by email

Write to info@autologic.online from the email address registered to your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will process your request within 14 days.

5. More information

For everything the App stores and how long we keep it, see section 11 of our Privacy Policy.
